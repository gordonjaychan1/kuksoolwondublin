What is Kuk Sool Won?

A complete martial art from Korea

Kuk Sool Won brings together the many fighting arts of Korea's history into one well-organized system. You learn to strike, kick, throw, lock joints and fall safely, and later train with traditional weapons.

It's not a sport. The focus is self-defense, a healthy body and a calm, strong mind, built on four values:

  • Humility
  • Self-discipline
  • Loyalty
  • Respect for others

What you'll learn

  1. Hand strikingOpen and closed hand strikes, blocks and parries
  2. KicksBasic, spinning, jumping and combination kicks
  3. Throws and grapplingBody and leg throws, ground fighting
  4. Joint locksControl, restraint and redirection
  5. Falls and acrobaticsHow to land safely, plus agility
  6. Body conditioningFlexibility, cardio and strength
  7. Animal stylesTiger, mantis, crane, dragon, snake
  8. Traditional weaponsSword, staff, spear, cane, rope and fan
  9. Breathing and meditationFocus, posture and breath control
  10. Healing methodsAcupressure and more, from instructor level

Chief instructor & owner

Master Seyd Saidi, 8th Dahn

Master Saidi started martial arts as a young boy in Afghanistan and trained in several styles up to black belt. He traveled through India performing demonstrations, then came to the United States, where he met Grandmaster In Hyuk Suh, founder of Kuk Sool Won.

He has taught in Dublin for over three decades. His goal has always been a safe, friendly school where families feel at home, and his students have earned several permanent state championship flags along the way.

State championship flags

Won three years in a row, kept for good

At each year's California State Championship, the World Kuk Sool Association awards a velvet flag to the school with the most points across all divisions. The winner holds it for one year.

Win it three years in a row, and the flag is yours to keep. Every permanent flag on our wall stands for one of those three-year streaks, earned by our students and instructors together.

Student creed

  1. I develop myself in a positive manner.
  2. I relate at all times with integrity and self-control.
  3. I will avoid anything that hinders my mental growth and physical health.
  4. I will use anything I learn in class constructively and defensively.

Before class

Good to know

  • Arrive 10 to 15 minutes early and change into your uniform right away
  • Keep your uniform neat and clean
  • Prepare quietly so you don't disturb the class on the mat
  • Show respect to your instructors and fellow students
  • Come regularly, and practice at home for faster progress
